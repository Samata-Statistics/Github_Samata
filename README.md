# Samata Das

**PhD Student in Statistics | Astrostatistics & Scientific Machine Learning**

I am a PhD student in Statistics at **The Pennsylvania State University**, working at the intersection of **statistics, machine learning, and astrophysics** as well as **statistics and bioinformatics along with latent variable explainability**. My research focuses on developing statistical and deep learning methods for extracting scientific information from complex astronomical and biological data.

## Research Interests

* Astrostatistics & Astroinformatics
* Gaussian processes and multivariate time series
* Simulation-based inference
* Neural likelihood estimation
* Deep learning and Flow based models for scientific inference
* Bayesian Deep Learning
* Structural bioinformatics
* Explainability
